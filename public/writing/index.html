Building calmer reserving workflows

Reserving is already hard. The goal of tooling should be to make the work calmer: fewer manual steps, fewer hidden assumptions, and fewer surprises at the end of the quarter. What I optimize for Reproducibility over heroics. Clear inputs and traceable assumptions. Small scripts that compose, not one giant platform. A simple loop Ingest data. Validate aggressively. Compute and explain. Ship a narrative, not just numbers. More soon.

A field guide to model reduction in insurance

Model reduction is a way to keep the shape of a complex system while throwing away most of its computational cost. In insurance, that mindset shows up as: Precomputing what can be precomputed. Using approximations with explicit error checks. Building workflows where the fast path is the default. Practical takeaway If you can write down what changes and what stays fixed, you can often build a reduced representation that is both faster and easier to explain. ...
