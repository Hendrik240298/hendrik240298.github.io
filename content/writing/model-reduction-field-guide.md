Model reduction is a way to keep the shape of a complex system while throwing away
most of its computational cost.

In insurance, that mindset shows up as:

- Precomputing what can be precomputed.
- Using approximations with explicit error checks.
- Building workflows where the fast path is the default.

## Practical takeaway

If you can write down what changes and what stays fixed, you can often build a
reduced representation that is both faster and easier to explain.
