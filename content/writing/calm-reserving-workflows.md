Reserving is already hard. The goal of tooling should be to make the work calmer:
fewer manual steps, fewer hidden assumptions, and fewer surprises at the end of the
quarter.

## What I optimize for

- Reproducibility over heroics.
- Clear inputs and traceable assumptions.
- Small scripts that compose, not one giant platform.

## A simple loop

1. Ingest data.
2. Validate aggressively.
3. Compute and explain.
4. Ship a narrative, not just numbers.
