## About

I work at the intersection of reserving, modeling, and automation, turning complex
data into calm, defensible decisions. My focus is on measurable impact: faster
analyses, clearer reporting, and scalable tooling for specialty lines.

Before insurance, I completed a PhD in numerical mathematics, researching reduced-
order models for physical systems. That research mindset now powers the actuarial
workflows I design in Python and SQL.

I care about simple systems, durable ideas, and professional curiosity. If a
solution cannot be explained in one page, it is not finished.
